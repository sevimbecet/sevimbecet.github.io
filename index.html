About

I started as an industrial engineer, planning production on a live factory floor, and moved into energy research because I wanted to work on systems that shape how cities run. Today I combine modeling, data and design to study transportation, buildings and districts as one energy system.

Before UC Davis, I was a research assistant at the Istanbul Technical University Energy Institute, working on EU-funded positive energy districts. I study in the U.S. with a graduate scholarship from the Republic of Türkiye Ministry of National Education.
